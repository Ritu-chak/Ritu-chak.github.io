about

PhD Researcher in Biomedical Sciences

With a Ph.D. from IIT Kharagpur and postdoctoral training at Johns Hopkins School of Medicine, I specialize in translational research on immune regulation in cardiovascular disease. My work focuses on the role of Natural Killer T cells in atherosclerosis progression and mechanisms underlying vascular inflammation.
