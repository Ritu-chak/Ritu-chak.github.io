01 / About

Scientific background in cardiovascular immunology.

I am a postdoctoral fellow at Johns Hopkins School of Medicine, where I investigate how the immune system shapes cardiovascular disease progression. My research focuses on the intersection of atherosclerosis, inflammation, and novel therapeutic strategies.

Throughout my career, I have been driven by questions about the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying chronic inflammatory diseases—particularly how lipid metabolism and immune dysregulation converge to fuel atherosclerotic plaque development.

02 / Research Pillars

Core research interests & methodologies.

🔬

Immune Cell Heterogeneity

Spectral flow cytometry and single-cell analysis to resolve myeloid and lymphoid subsets in atherosclerotic tissue.

📊

Lipid Metabolism

Investigating how cholesterol homeostasis and lipid sensing pathways influence immune cell fate and function.

🧬

Epigenetic Regulation

Exploring histone acetylation and DNA methylation patterns as drivers of pro-inflammatory gene expression.

💊

Drug Target Discovery

Identifying and validating novel immunological targets for cardiovascular disease prevention and reversal.

🔗

Mechanistic Pathways

Dissecting intracellular signaling cascades linking metabolic cues to adaptive immune responses.

🌍

Translational Models

Moving discoveries from cell culture through in vivo models to human clinical correlates.
